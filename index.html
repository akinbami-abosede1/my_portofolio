About Me

Who I am and what I do

I am a Data Analyst with a strong interest in using data to understand problems, uncover patterns and support informed decision-making.

I hold a B.Sc. in Information and Communication Science from the University of Ilorin, where I graduated with First-Class Honours and a CGPA of 4.81.

My experience spans data analysis, business intelligence,statistical analysis and machine learning. I have worked on projects involving data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, statistical modelling and interactive dashboard development. I enjoy working with datasets and translating analytical findings into insights that are easy to understand and useful in practice.

I am committed to continuous learning and improving my ability to apply data-driven approaches to real-world problems.

Selected Data Analytics Projects

Cancer Risk Analysis and Visualization

An interactive Power BI dashboard analysing 2,000 records across cancer types and risk levels.

Objective
Explore cancer distribution, risk levels and associated factors.
Approach
Examined lifestyle, dietary, health, genetic and environmental factors using interactive visuals and filters.
Key Findings
Medium-risk cases formed the largest group (1,574). Lung cancer had the highest representation, while skin cancer had the lowest.
  • Power BI
  • Data Visualization
  • Dashboard Development

Diabetes Prediction and Classification

A machine learning project comparing classification models for diabetes prediction.

Objective
Compare prediction models and assess their performance on unseen data.
Approach
Cleaned and explored data with Python and Pandas, applied SMOTE, and compared six classification models.
Key Findings
Random Forest performed best among the models evaluated. Model evaluation also helped identify potential overfitting.
  • Python
  • Pandas
  • SMOTE
  • XGBoost
View on GitHub

Pizza Sales Analysis using SQL

An analysis of approximately 48,000 pizza sales records.

Objective
Understand sales patterns, product performance and trends.
Approach
Used SQL aggregation and comparison techniques to analyse sales across products.
Key Findings
Identified stronger and weaker performing products to support sales-related decisions.
